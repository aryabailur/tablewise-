# Saffron Terrace — Policies

## Reservations

Book online, through the Tablewise concierge or by phone (+91 22 4122 6600). Seatings every 30 minutes from 12:00 pm, last seating 9:30 pm; tables are held for 90 minutes. Booking codes start with SAF (for example SAF-9T3D).

## Party Size

1 to 12 guests online. Groups of 8–12 are seated at the large rooftop tables or in the Diwan-e-Khas private room; set menus from ₹1,600 per person.

## Cancellations

Please cancel at least 12 hours ahead. Weekend ghazal-night bookings for 6 or more guests that are cancelled late or no-show are charged ₹500 per guest.

## Seating Zones

**Rooftop** terrace with lake views (2–8 guests, open-air; covered during monsoon), **Window** tables in the air-conditioned dining room (2–4), **Garden** courtyard (4) and **Private** (12). Rooftop tables are the most requested, so book early for weekends.

## Children & Accessibility

Families are very welcome and high chairs are available. Lift access to the rooftop; accessible restroom on the terrace level.

## Payment

Cards and UPI accepted. No service charge; tipping is optional. Outside cakes are allowed with a ₹300 cake-cutting fee.
