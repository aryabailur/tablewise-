## Allergens & Dietary Notes

(V) vegetarian · (VG) vegan · (GF) gluten-free. Many gravies use cashew paste and cream. Please tell us about nut allergies when booking; nut-free versions of butter chicken and paneer are possible with notice. A Jain menu (no onion, garlic or root vegetables) is available at lunch.
