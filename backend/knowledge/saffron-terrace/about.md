# Saffron Terrace — About

Saffron Terrace serves slow-cooked North Indian and Mughlai food on a rooftop terrace overlooking Powai Lake. Chef Iqbal Qureshi's family recipes come from Lucknow and Old Delhi: dum biryanis sealed with dough, galouti kebabs and breads from a clay tandoor. Average cost for two: ₹2,400 before taxes.

## Location & Getting Here

Rooftop, Lakeside Arcade, Hiranandani Gardens, Powai, Mumbai 400076. About 15 minutes from Kanjurmarg station or the JVLR. Free basement parking for diners (validate your ticket at the host stand).

## Opening Hours

- **Open:** 12:00 pm – 11:00 pm daily (lunch menu until 3:30 pm, dinner menu from 7:00 pm, kebabs and chaat in between).
- Bookings are accepted from 12:00 pm through the last seating at 9:30 pm; the kitchen closes at 10:45 pm.

## Live Music

- **Friday & Saturday — Ghazal Nights:** live ghazals and sufi music from 8:30 pm on the terrace.
- **Sunday — Qawwali Brunch:** 12:30 pm, ₹1,800 per person with unlimited kebabs and biryani.

## Private Dining

The Diwan-e-Khas private room seats up to 12 with a dedicated server; minimum spend ₹18,000.
