# Tulsi Thali House — Policies

## Reservations

Book online, through the Tablewise concierge or on +91 22 2671 4455. Seatings every 30 minutes from 11:30 am with last seating at 9:00 pm; tables are held for 90 minutes. Booking codes start with TUL (for example TUL-5C7N). Walk-ins are always welcome, though weekend lunches have a queue.

## Party Size

1 to 12 guests online. The garden court has tables for 4–8; the private hall seats 12. For groups over 12 please call us.

## Cancellations

No cancellation fee. Please cancel through Tablewise or call us so we can give the table to another family. Tables are held for 15 minutes after the booking time.

## Seating Zones

**Window** (2–4 guests, air-conditioned), **Garden** court (4–8 guests, covered, fans) and **Private** hall (12).

## House Rules

Pure vegetarian premises: please do not bring non-vegetarian food, eggs or alcohol. Outside birthday cakes must be eggless (₹150 cake-cutting fee). No smoking.

## Children & Accessibility

Children are welcome at all times; under-3s eat free. Step-free entrance and a wheelchair-accessible restroom.

## Payment

Cash, cards and UPI accepted. Prices include GST; no service charge.
