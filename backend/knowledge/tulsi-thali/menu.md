# Tulsi Thali House — Menu

Prices in INR, inclusive of GST. 100% pure vegetarian; no eggs, onion-garlic-free (Jain) versions available for every item.

## Thalis (unlimited refills)

- **Tulsi Maharaja Thali** — weekday lunch ₹449, dinner and weekends ₹549. Includes 4 farsan, 4 shaak (vegetables), 2 dals and kadhi, rotli, bajra rotla, puri, khichdi, rice, 2 sweets, chaas, papad and chutneys.
- **Rajasthani Shahi Thali** — ₹599. Dal-baati-churma, gatte ki sabzi, ker sangri, bajra khichdi, missi roti, ghevar (seasonal).
- **Kids' Mini Thali** (under 10) — ₹249. Two shaak, dal, rotli, rice, one sweet.
- **Jain Thali** — same price as the Maharaja thali; no onion, garlic, potato or other root vegetables.

## Sample Daily Rotation

- **Farsan:** dhokla, khandvi, patra, methi gota, kachori.
- **Shaak:** undhiyu (winter), sev tameta, bhindi sambhariya, ringna bataka, paneer kofta.
- **Dal & Kadhi:** sweet-and-sour Gujarati dal, Gujarati kadhi, panchmel dal.
- **Sweets:** shrikhand, aamras (April–June), mohanthal, basundi, moong dal halwa, malpua.

## Allergens & Dietary Notes

Our kitchen uses dairy and ghee extensively, gluten (wheat rotli, puri, baati), gram flour, peanuts (in several shaak and chutneys), sesame, cashew and almonds. Tell your server about allergies and we will mark your thali; peanut-free and dairy-free (oil-based) thalis can be prepared with 2 hours' notice. Vegan guests should ask for the oil-based thali without ghee, chaas or milk sweets.
