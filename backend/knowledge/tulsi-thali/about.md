# Tulsi Thali House — About

Tulsi Thali House is a pure-vegetarian Gujarati and Rajasthani thali restaurant in Andheri West, run by the Parekh family since 1998. Servers in traditional safas bring more than 20 preparations to your silver thali and keep refilling until you say stop. No eggs, meat, fish or alcohol are ever brought onto the premises. Average cost for two: ₹900 before taxes.

## Location & Getting Here

Shop 4–6, Navrang Chambers, S. V. Road, Andheri West, Mumbai 400058 — a 5-minute walk from Andheri station (Western line) and 3 minutes from the Andheri metro. Paid parking in the building compound (₹50).

## Opening Hours

- **Open:** 11:30 am – 10:30 pm daily; last seating at 9:00 pm.
- **Lunch thali:** 11:30 am – 3:30 pm. **Dinner thali:** 7:00 pm onwards. Light snacks (dhokla, khandvi, chaas) are served in between.
- Special festive thalis during Navratri, Diwali and Makar Sankranti.

## Families & Groups

Tulsi is a family favourite: high chairs, a kids' mini thali and large garden-court tables for joint families. The private hall seats 12 for birthdays and kitty parties.
