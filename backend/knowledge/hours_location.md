# Hours, Location & Getting Here

## Opening Hours

Ember & Vine is open seven nights a week for dinner.

- **Dinner service:** 5:00 pm – 11:00 pm daily, last seating at 10:00 pm.
- **Kitchen:** closes at 10:30 pm; a late snack menu runs at the bar until 11:00 pm.
- **Wine bar:** open until midnight on Friday and Saturday.
- **Sunday Hearth Brunch:** 11:00 am – 2:30 pm (walk-in only, not bookable online).
- **Holidays:** open on most holidays. Closed on Christmas Day and New Year's Day. Special menus run on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve.

## Address

214 Larkspur Lane, Hudson Quarter, San Francisco, CA 94107. We are on the corner of Larkspur Lane and Vine Street, opposite the old tram depot — look for the copper lanterns and the open fire in the window.

## Parking

Valet parking is available Thursday to Sunday from 5:30 pm for $18. Self-parking is available at the Hudson Quarter Garage at 180 Vine Street (2 minutes' walk), which offers a $12 evening flat rate after 5 pm. Validate your ticket with our host for a $5 discount. Street parking is metered until 9:00 pm. We have two EV charging spaces in the Hudson Quarter Garage on level 1.

## Public Transport

We are a 6-minute walk from the Hudson Quarter BART station (exit B, Vine Street). Muni lines 10 and 47 stop at Larkspur & Vine directly outside. Bike racks are available by the garden gate.
