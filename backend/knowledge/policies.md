# Reservation Policies

## Making a Reservation

Reservations open 60 days in advance and can be made online, through our AI concierge, or by phone at (415) 555-0142. Tables are seated every 30 minutes from 5:00 pm, with last seating at 10:00 pm. A standard reservation holds the table for 90 minutes; tasting-menu reservations hold the table for 2.5 hours. We keep a portion of the bar and garden for walk-ins each night.

## Party Size

Online and concierge bookings accept parties of 1 to 12 guests. Parties of 7–12 are seated in our garden long tables or private rooms and are asked to pre-select from a set menu at least 48 hours ahead. Parties larger than 12, or anyone wishing to book the entire restaurant, should contact our events team at events@emberandvine.com.

## Cancellations & No-Shows

Please cancel or modify at least 24 hours before your reservation. For parties of 1–6 there is no charge for late cancellation, but repeated no-shows may affect future bookings. For parties of 7 or more, and for all tasting-menu bookings, a card guarantee is required and a fee of $50 per guest applies to cancellations within 24 hours or no-shows. You can cancel instantly with your booking code (for example EV-7K2P) via the concierge.

## Late Arrivals

We hold tables for 15 minutes past the reservation time. If you are running late, please call us — we will do our best to keep your table, though we may need to shorten your seating or move you to the bar.

## Seating Zones

We have four seating zones. **Window** tables (2–4 guests) look onto the lantern-lit street. The **Garden** is a covered, heated courtyard with olive trees, ideal for groups (2–8 guests). The **Bar** counter seats up to 2 per spot with a front-row view of the hearth. **Private** rooms (the Cellar Room, the Vine Room and the Hearth Room) seat 6, 10 and 12 guests. Zone requests are honoured whenever possible but are not guaranteed.

## Corkage

You are welcome to bring a special bottle. Corkage is $35 per 750 ml bottle, limited to two bottles per table. We waive corkage for one bottle for every bottle purchased from our list. We kindly ask that you do not bring wines that are already on our list.

## Dress Code

Smart casual. We ask that guests avoid athletic wear and beachwear. Jackets are not required.

## Children

Children are welcome until 8:00 pm. High chairs are available — please mention them in your booking notes. We offer half portions of most mains.

## Gratuity & Payment

A discretionary 20% service charge is added for parties of 7 or more. We accept all major cards and Apple/Google Pay. We are a cashless restaurant.

## Gift Cards

Gift cards are available in any amount from $50, online or at the host stand, and are valid for five years.

## Accessibility

The dining room, garden and restrooms are fully step-free and wheelchair accessible. The bar counter has two lowered seats. Service animals are always welcome.
