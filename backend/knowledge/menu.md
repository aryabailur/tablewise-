# Ember & Vine Dinner Menu

Ember & Vine is a wood-fire restaurant and natural wine bar. Everything on the menu passes through, over, or near our 4-metre oak and almond-wood hearth. Menus change with the seasons; this is the autumn menu, served nightly from 5:00 pm. Executive Chef: Marisol Achterberg. Pastry Chef: Tomás Ilunga.

## Tasting Menu

The **Chef's Hearth Tasting Menu** is seven courses that showcase the best of the season. $135 per person, with optional wine pairing ($85) or zero-proof pairing ($55). A vegetarian tasting menu is available at the same price, and a fully vegan tasting menu is available with 48 hours' notice. The tasting menu must be ordered by the entire table and is served until 9:00 pm.

## Wine & Drinks

Our cellar holds roughly 400 labels with a focus on natural, organic and biodynamic producers. Over 30 wines are offered by the glass, including skin-contact (orange) wines, pét-nats and rare Coravin pours.

- **Signature cocktails** ($16–19): *Smoke & Mirrors* (mezcal, Aperol, grapefruit, smoked salt rim), *Vine Spritz* (orange wine, elderflower, soda), *Ember Old Fashioned* (bourbon, smoked maple, walnut bitters).
- **Zero-proof** ($10–12): *Garden Tonic* (cucumber, basil, verjus, tonic), *Smoked Cherry Fizz*.
- **Beer & cider**: local craft rotations on draught.

## Dietary Key

(V) vegetarian · (VG) vegan · (GF) gluten-free · (DF) dairy-free. Many dishes can be adapted; please tell your server about any dietary needs. See the allergens guide for full details.

## Vegan Options

Vegan guests are very welcome. Fully vegan dishes on the current menu: Charred Padrón Peppers, Smoked Beetroot Carpaccio, Charcoal Cauliflower, Ember-Roasted Squash, Charred Broccolini and the Fire-Roasted Pear dessert. Our Ember Bread can be served with olive oil instead of butter. A seven-course vegan tasting menu is available with 48 hours' notice — add a note to your booking.
