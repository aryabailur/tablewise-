# Tablewise — Frequently Asked Questions

Tablewise is a restaurant discovery and table booking service for Mumbai. You can browse restaurants, check live availability and book a table online or by chatting with the Tablewise concierge. Bookings are free; Tablewise never charges guests.

## Which restaurants are on Tablewise?

Six restaurants across Mumbai: Ember & Vine (wood-fire and modern European, Lower Parel), Trattoria Alba (Italian and pizza with a rooftop, Bandra), Saffron Terrace (North Indian and Mughlai rooftop, Powai), Koli & Tide (coastal Malvani seafood, Juhu), Kanji House (Japanese and pan-Asian, Colaba) and Tulsi Thali House (pure-veg Gujarati and Rajasthani thalis, Andheri).

## How do I book?

Pick a restaurant, choose a date, party size and one of the open time slots, then add your name and a phone number or email. You can also ask the concierge, for example "a rooftop table for 4 in Bandra on Saturday at 8". Every booking gets a code made of the restaurant's prefix and four characters, such as EMB-7K2P or ALB-4M8Q.

## How do I change or cancel a booking?

Use your booking code on the "My booking" page or tell the concierge the code. Cancellation windows and any late-cancellation fees are set by each restaurant; check its policies page.

## What do the price levels mean?

Price levels run from ₹ to ₹₹₹₹ and are based on the average cost for two before drinks: ₹ is under ₹1,000, ₹₹ about ₹1,000–2,000, ₹₹₹ about ₹2,000–3,500 and ₹₹₹₹ above ₹3,500.

## How big a group can I book?

Online and concierge bookings are for 1 to 12 guests. For larger groups contact the restaurant directly.

## Can I choose where I sit?

You can request a seating zone (window, garden, bar, private, rooftop or patio, depending on the restaurant). Zones are honoured whenever possible.

## Do you handle allergies?

Add allergies to your booking notes and tell your server on arrival. Each restaurant's menu page lists common allergens, but kitchens handle many ingredients and cannot guarantee trace-free food.

## Is my data shared?

Your name and contact details are shared only with the restaurant you book, to manage your reservation.
