# Events & Private Dining

## Private Dining Rooms

We have three private rooms, each with its own sound system and screen:

- **The Cellar Room** — seats up to 6 among our wine racks. Minimum spend $600 Sunday–Thursday, $900 Friday–Saturday.
- **The Vine Room** — seats up to 10 under a canopy of living vines. Minimum spend $1,200 Sunday–Thursday, $1,800 Friday–Saturday.
- **The Hearth Room** — seats up to 12 with a private view of the main hearth. Minimum spend $1,500 Sunday–Thursday, $2,400 Friday–Saturday.

Private rooms can be booked online for up to 12 guests; our events team will contact you within 24 hours to arrange the menu. Full buyouts (up to 120 guests standing, 80 seated) are available on request.

- **Thursday — Chef's Counter:** eight seats at the hearth counter for an improvised 10-course menu with Chef Marisol. $165 per person, 7:30 pm sharp. Book via the bar zone with a note.
- **Sunday — Hearth Brunch:** wood-fired brunch from 11:00 am, walk-in only.
