# Kanji House — Policies

## Reservations

Book online, through the Tablewise concierge or on +91 22 2284 0909. Seatings every 30 minutes from 7:00 pm, last seating 10:00 pm; tables are held for 90 minutes (omakase seatings run 2 hours). Booking codes start with KNJ (for example KNJ-8F5R).

## Party Size

1 to 12 guests online, but the room is small: the largest table seats 6 (the private tatami room). The sushi counter seats pairs. For groups over 6 please call us.

## Cancellations & No-Shows

Cancel at least 24 hours ahead. Omakase seats are prepaid and non-refundable within 24 hours, but can be transferred to another guest. Other no-shows are charged ₹1,500 per guest.

## Seating Zones

**Bar** — the nine-seat sushi and robata counter (pairs); **Window** — tables overlooking Walton Road (2–4); **Private** — the tatami room (up to 6, shoes off, low seating with leg wells).

## Age & Dress

Guests must be 25 or older to be served alcohol; children under 12 are welcome only before 8:30 pm. Smart casual; no flip-flops after 8:00 pm.

## Accessibility

Kanji House is on the first floor of a heritage building with no lift (18 steps). Please call ahead and we will do our best to help.

## Payment

Cards and UPI. A 10% service charge applies to all bills and is clearly shown. Corkage ₹3,000 per bottle (sake and wine only).
