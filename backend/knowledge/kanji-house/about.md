# Kanji House — About

Kanji House is an intimate Japanese izakaya and omakase counter tucked into a heritage building in Colaba. Chef Kenji Morimoto-Rao (half Japanese, half Mangalorean) runs a binchotan robata grill, a nine-seat sushi counter and one of Mumbai's deepest Japanese whisky lists. Average cost for two: ₹4,800 before taxes and drinks.

## Location & Getting Here

1st floor, Ralli House, 22 Walton Road, Colaba, Mumbai 400001 — two minutes from the Regal Cinema circle. Churchgate station is 12 minutes by taxi. Paid public parking at the Colaba Causeway lot; valet from 8:00 pm for ₹250.

## Opening Hours

- **Dinner:** 7:00 pm – midnight, seven nights a week.
- Last seating at 10:00 pm (bookings are taken until 10:29 pm on the hour or half hour); the robata closes at 11:15 pm; the bar pours until midnight.

## Omakase

The **Kanji Omakase** at the sushi counter (bar zone) is 14 courses at ₹7,500 per person, two seatings at 7:30 pm and 9:30 pm. Fish is flown in from Toyosu twice a week and complemented by Indian west-coast catch. Please book the bar zone and add "omakase" to your notes.
