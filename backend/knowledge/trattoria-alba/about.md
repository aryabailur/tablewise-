# Trattoria Alba — About

Trattoria Alba is a neighbourhood Italian trattoria in Bandra West with a wood-fired pizza oven, hand-rolled pasta made every morning and a breezy rooftop terrace with a spritz bar. Chef-owner Giulia Ferraro trained in Bologna and Naples before moving to Mumbai. Average cost for two: ₹2,800 before taxes and drinks.

## Location & Getting Here

3rd floor and rooftop, Casa Verde, 14 Pali Mala Road, Bandra West, Mumbai 400050. Bandra station (Western line) is a 12-minute auto ride. Street parking is limited; valet is available from 7:00 pm for ₹150.

## Opening Hours

- **Lunch & dinner:** 12:00 pm – 11:30 pm daily, last seating at 10:00 pm.
- **Pizza oven:** fires all day; the kitchen closes at 11:00 pm.
- **Aperitivo hour:** 5:00 pm – 7:00 pm on the rooftop, with half-price spritzes.

## Rooftop & Pets

The rooftop seats about 40 under a retractable awning that stays open in the dry season and closes during monsoon showers. Well-behaved dogs are welcome on the rooftop and at window tables; we keep water bowls and a small dog menu (plain grilled chicken, ₹250).
