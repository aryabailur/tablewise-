## Allergens & Dietary Notes

(V) vegetarian · (VG) vegan · (GF) gluten-free · (DF) dairy-free. Our kitchen uses gluten, dairy, egg, tree nuts, shellfish, fish and celery. The pizza oven and fryers are shared, so we cannot guarantee coeliac-safe preparation. Jain pizzas and pastas (no onion, garlic or root vegetables) are available on request.
