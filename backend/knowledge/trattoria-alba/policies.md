# Trattoria Alba — Policies

## Reservations

Book online, through the Tablewise concierge or on +91 22 6150 7788. Seatings every 30 minutes from 12:00 pm with last seating at 10:00 pm; tables are held for 90 minutes. Booking codes start with ALB (for example ALB-4M8Q). We keep the bar for walk-ins.

## Party Size

Online bookings for 1 to 12 guests. Groups of 8–12 are seated at the rooftop long table or in the private room (10 guests) with a set menu from ₹1,800 per person.

## Cancellations

Free cancellation up to 6 hours before your booking. Rooftop tables on Friday and Saturday evenings cancelled later than that, or no-shows, may be charged ₹500 per guest.

## Seating Zones

**Rooftop** (2–8 guests, open-air, dog-friendly), **Window** (2–4 guests, indoors with air-conditioning), **Bar** (pairs at the spritz bar) and **Private** (one room for up to 10). During heavy monsoon rain rooftop guests are moved indoors where possible.

## Pets

Dogs are welcome on the rooftop and at window tables on a leash. Please mention your dog in the booking notes so we can seat you appropriately.

## Children & Accessibility

Children are welcome at all times; high chairs and a kids' pasta menu are available. A lift serves the rooftop and the 3rd-floor restrooms are wheelchair accessible.

## Payment & Corkage

Cards and UPI accepted. No service charge is added; tips are at your discretion. Corkage ₹1,500 per bottle. Alcohol is served only to guests aged 25 and above.
