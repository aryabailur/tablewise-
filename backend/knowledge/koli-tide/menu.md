## Allergens

Our kitchen handles fish, shellfish, molluscs, gluten (rava, wheat), dairy and peanuts. Most curries are naturally gluten-free and dairy-free; coconut is used widely, so please tell us about coconut or tree-nut allergies. Shared fryers are used for seafood and vegetarian starters.
