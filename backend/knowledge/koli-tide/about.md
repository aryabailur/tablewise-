# Koli & Tide — About

Koli & Tide celebrates the coastal cooking of Mumbai's Koli fishing community and the Konkan and Malvan coast. The day's catch arrives from Versova and Sassoon Docks every morning and is cooked in recipes from Chef Sunita Tandel's grandmother: Malvani masalas, kokum, coconut and fiery Koli chutneys. Average cost for two: ₹1,800 before taxes.

## Location & Getting Here

Ground floor, Sea Breeze Bungalow, 9 Gandhigram Road, Juhu, Mumbai 400049 — a 3-minute walk from Juhu beach. Vile Parle station (Western line) is 10 minutes by auto. Limited parking in the lane; valet on Friday to Sunday evenings for ₹150.

## Opening Hours

- **Open:** 12:00 pm – 11:30 pm daily; last seating at 10:00 pm.
- **Fish thali lunch:** 12:00 pm – 3:30 pm on weekdays.
- **Bar:** feni, toddy-inspired cocktails and cold beer until 11:30 pm.

## The Patio & Pets

Our shaded garden patio catches the sea breeze. Dogs are welcome on the patio; we serve them water and plain boiled fish on request.

## Events

- **Thursday — Catch of the Day Auction:** at 8:00 pm the chef presents the best of the day's catch and tables bid for it in good fun.
- **Narali Purnima (August):** a coconut-themed festival menu.
