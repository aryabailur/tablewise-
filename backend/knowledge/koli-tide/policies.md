# Koli & Tide — Policies

## Reservations

Book online, through the Tablewise concierge or on +91 22 2620 3344. Seatings every 30 minutes from 12:00 pm, last seating 10:00 pm; tables are held for 90 minutes. Booking codes start with KOL (for example KOL-2H6W). Walk-ins are welcome at the bar.

## Party Size

1 to 12 guests online. The private room seats up to 10 and is ideal for family lunches; the patio long table seats 6.

## Cancellations

Free cancellation up to 3 hours before your booking. Large parties (8+) who cancel later or don't show may be charged ₹300 per guest.

## Seating Zones

**Patio** (2–6 guests, shaded garden patio, dog-friendly), **Window** (2–4 guests, air-conditioned dining room), **Bar** (pairs) and **Private** (10). The patio may close in heavy monsoon rain; we will move you indoors.

## Pets

Dogs are welcome on the patio on a leash. Please note it in your booking.

## Children & Accessibility

Children welcome; high chairs available and a mild kids' fish-and-rice plate (₹245). The ground floor is step-free; the restroom has a grab rail but a narrow door.

## Payment

Cards and UPI accepted. No service charge. No corkage — we do not allow outside alcohol.
