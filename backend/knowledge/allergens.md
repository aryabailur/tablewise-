# Allergens & Dietary Requirements

We take allergies seriously. Please tell us about any allergy when booking (add it to your booking notes) and remind your server when you are seated. Our kitchen handles all 14 major allergens, so while we take great care to prevent cross-contact, we cannot guarantee any dish is completely free of trace allergens.

## Gluten & Coeliac

Most of our menu is naturally gluten-free because it is cooked over open fire rather than fried or breaded. Gluten-free dishes are marked (GF) on the menu. We bake gluten-free bread daily and can serve it in place of Ember Bread. We do not have a dedicated gluten-free fryer; the Ember Chips served with the ribeye are cooked in shared oil, so coeliac guests should swap them for Ember Potatoes. Our flatbreads, Hearth S'mores and Bone Marrow toast contain gluten.

## Nuts & Peanuts

We use tree nuts (almonds, hazelnuts, pistachios, walnuts) in several dishes and our hearth burns almond wood, but we do not use peanuts or peanut oil anywhere in the kitchen. Nut-containing dishes include: Charred Padrón Peppers (smoked almonds), Burrata (hazelnut), Smoked Beetroot Carpaccio (pistachio), Seared Scallops (hazelnut), Charred Broccolini (almonds), the Cheese Board (walnut bread) and the Ember Old Fashioned (walnut bitters). Most can be prepared without the nuts — ask your server.

## Dairy

Dairy-free dishes are marked (DF). Our vegan dishes are all dairy-free. Butter is used to finish many grilled items; we can finish with olive oil instead on request.

## Shellfish & Fish

Shellfish appears in the Oysters on the Coals, Grilled Octopus (mollusc) and Seared Scallops. Fish appears in the Branzino, Market Fish and our salsa verde (contains anchovy). Our shellfish is cooked on a separate grill section, but the hearth is shared.

## Eggs, Sesame & Soy

Eggs are used in the Beef Tartare, aioli, cheesecake and chocolate cake. Sesame is used in the tahini (Beetroot Carpaccio). Soy is present in the miso caramel (Charcoal Cauliflower).

## Vegetarian & Vegan

Vegetarian dishes are marked (V) and vegan dishes (VG). We use vegetable stock for our risotto and no animal rennet in our parmesan alternative on request. A vegetarian tasting menu is always available; a vegan tasting menu requires 48 hours' notice.

## Other Requirements

We can accommodate pregnancy-safe, halal (chicken and lamb are halal-certified on request with 24 hours' notice), low-FODMAP and alliums-free requests with advance notice. Children's portions are available for most mains.
