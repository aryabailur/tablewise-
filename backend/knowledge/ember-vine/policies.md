# Ember & Vine — Policies

## Reservations

Reservations open 60 days in advance online, through the Tablewise concierge, or by phone at +91 22 4890 1142. Tables are seated every 30 minutes from 6:00 pm, with last seating at 10:00 pm. A standard reservation holds the table for 90 minutes; tasting-menu reservations hold it for 2.5 hours. A portion of the bar is kept for walk-ins each night. Booking codes start with EMB (for example EMB-7K2P).

## Party Size

Online bookings accept parties of 1 to 12 guests. Parties of 7–12 are seated in the garden or a private room and pre-select a set menu 48 hours ahead. Larger groups should email events@emberandvine.in.

## Cancellations & No-Shows

Please cancel or modify at least 24 hours before your reservation. Parties of 7 or more and all tasting-menu bookings require a card guarantee; a fee of ₹2,000 per guest applies to late cancellations or no-shows.

## Late Arrivals

Tables are held for 15 minutes past the reservation time. Please call if you are running late.

## Seating Zones

**Window** tables (2–4 guests) overlook the mill courtyard. The **Garden** is a covered, fan-cooled courtyard (4–6 guests). The **Bar** counter seats pairs with a front-row view of the hearth. **Private** rooms seat 6, 10 and 12. Zone requests are honoured when possible but not guaranteed.

## Corkage

Corkage is ₹2,500 per 750 ml bottle, maximum two bottles per table, waived for one bottle for every bottle bought from our list.

## Dress Code, Children & Accessibility

Smart casual; please avoid beachwear and sports shorts. Children are welcome until 8:30 pm and high chairs are available on request. The dining room, garden and restrooms are step-free and wheelchair accessible.

## Payment

A discretionary 10% service charge is added for parties of 7 or more. We accept all major cards and UPI. Prices exclude 5% GST. Guests must be 25+ to be served alcohol, as per Maharashtra excise rules.
