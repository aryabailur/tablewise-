## Tasting Menu

The **Chef's Hearth Tasting Menu** is seven courses at ₹6,000 per person, with optional wine pairing (₹4,000) or zero-proof pairing (₹2,200). A vegetarian version is available at the same price; a fully vegan tasting menu needs 48 hours' notice. The whole table must order it and it is served until 9:00 pm.

## Wine & Drinks

Around 300 labels with a focus on natural, organic and biodynamic producers, including Indian natural wines from Nashik. Over 25 wines by the glass from ₹900.

- **Signature cocktails** (₹850–1,100): *Smoke & Mirrors* (mezcal, Aperol, grapefruit, smoked salt), *Vine Spritz* (orange wine, elderflower, soda), *Ember Old Fashioned* (bourbon, smoked jaggery, walnut bitters).
- **Zero-proof** (₹450–550): *Garden Tonic* (cucumber, basil, verjus), *Smoked Kokum Fizz*.

## Dietary Key & Allergens

(V) vegetarian · (VG) vegan · (GF) gluten-free · (DF) dairy-free. Most of the menu is naturally gluten-free because it is cooked over fire rather than fried. Our kitchen handles all major allergens (gluten, dairy, egg, peanuts, tree nuts, sesame, soy, fish, shellfish), so we cannot guarantee any dish is free of traces. Jain preparations (no onion, garlic or root vegetables) are possible for the risotto and several small plates with notice.
