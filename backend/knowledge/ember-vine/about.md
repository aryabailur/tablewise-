# Ember & Vine — About

Ember & Vine is a wood-fire restaurant and natural wine bar in a restored textile-mill compound in Lower Parel, Mumbai. Everything on the menu passes through, over, or near our 4-metre hearth fired with babul and mango wood. Executive Chef: Marisol Achterberg. Head Sommelier: Inès Delacroix. Average cost for two: ₹4,200 before taxes and drinks.

## Location & Getting Here

Unit 7, Old Sunrise Mill Compound, Senapati Bapat Marg, Lower Parel, Mumbai 400013. The nearest local station is Lower Parel (Western line), a 6-minute walk; Mahalaxmi is 10 minutes away. Valet parking is available at the compound gate from 6:00 pm for ₹200. Ride-share drop-off is at Gate 2.

## Opening Hours

- **Dinner:** 6:00 pm – 11:30 pm daily, last seating at 10:00 pm.
- **Kitchen:** closes at 11:00 pm; a short bar-snack menu runs until close.
- **Sunday Hearth Brunch:** 12:00 pm – 3:30 pm (walk-in only, not bookable online).
- Open on most public holidays; closed on Diwali (Lakshmi Puja night) and 1 January.

## Live Music & Events

- **Tuesday — Natural Wine Flights:** three 90 ml pours chosen by our sommelier for ₹1,800 at the bar.
- **Chef's Counter (Wednesdays):** eight seats at the hearth for a 10-course improvised menu, ₹6,500 per person, 7:30 pm sharp. Book the bar zone with a note.
- **Diwali Hearth Feast** — Saturday 7 November 2026, a six-course festive menu at ₹5,500 per person.

## Private Dining

Three private rooms with their own sound system: the Cellar Room (6 guests), the Vine Room (10) and the Hearth Room (12). Minimum spend from ₹30,000 Sunday–Thursday and ₹45,000 Friday–Saturday. Full buyouts up to 80 seated guests can be arranged through events@emberandvine.in.
