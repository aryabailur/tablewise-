# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes. We keep about a third of the bar counter and several garden tables for walk-ins each evening. Weeknights before 6:30 pm are usually the easiest time to walk in. On Friday and Saturday we recommend booking ahead.

## Can I request a specific table?

You can request a seating zone (window, garden, bar or private) when booking and we will do our best to honour it. Specific table numbers cannot be guaranteed, but add a note and our host will try.

## Is the garden heated? What happens if it rains?

The garden is fully covered with a retractable glass roof and infrared heaters, so it is open year-round in any weather. Blankets are available on cooler nights.

## Do you cater for large groups?

Yes — parties up to 12 can book online. Groups of 7 or more choose from a set menu and a discretionary 20% service charge applies. Groups larger than 12 should email events@emberandvine.com.

## How do I modify or cancel my booking?

Use your booking code (it looks like EV-7K2P) with our AI concierge or on the website. You can change the date, time or party size, subject to availability. Please give at least 24 hours' notice.

## Is there a tasting menu?

Yes — the seven-course Chef's Hearth Tasting Menu is $135 per person with optional wine pairing ($85). It must be ordered by the whole table and is served until 9:00 pm.

## Can I bring my own wine?

Yes, corkage is $35 per bottle, maximum two bottles per table, and waived one-for-one when you buy a bottle from our list.

## Are dogs allowed?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome in the garden. Service animals are welcome everywhere.

## Do you have a dress code?

Smart casual — no athletic wear or beachwear, but jackets are not required.

## Is the restaurant noisy?

The main dining room has a lively, warm atmosphere (around 70 dB at peak). For quieter conversations request a window table or the garden; private rooms are the quietest.

## Who runs Ember & Vine?

Ember & Vine was opened in 2019 by Chef Marisol Achterberg and sommelier Inès Delacroix. It holds a Michelin Bib Gourmand (2024–2026) and was named one of the Bay Area's best new wine bars by the Chronicle.

## Do you offer gift cards?

Yes, from $50, online or at the host stand. They are valid for five years and can be used for dinner, events and brunch.
